# Still Disease

- **Autoinflammatory**
  - Not an autoimmune condition
  - Occur in the absence of autoantibodies
- **Cytokine Storm**
  - Interleukins excessively produced
  - Massive inflammation
- 16-35 years: Adult Onset Still Disease (AOSD)
- \< 16 years: Systemic Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis (sJIA)
- Can be monophasic (single episode), polycyclic (recurrent), or chronic (persistent arthritis)

## Symptoms

> FART Pain

- **Fever**
  - ≥ 39°C
  - Often spiking once or twice daily
- **Arthritis/Arthralgia**
  - Occur in all patients
  - Polyarthritis: Wrists, kness, ankles most affected
- **Rash**:
  - Salmon-pink, maculopapular
  - Often comes and goes with fever spikes
  - "Evanescent rash"
- **Sore throat/Pharyngitis**:
  - Severe, non-suppurative
  - Common early symptom
  - Precedes the fever
- **Musle pain**:
  - Worsens with fever
- **Others**:
  - Lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, pleuritis, pericarditis

## Complications

- **Joint**: Joint destruction (similar to rheumatoid arthritis)
- **Heart**: Pericarditis, myocarditis
- **Lung**: Pleural effusion, interstitial lung disease
- **ICU**: Macrophage activation syndrome (MAS) or Hemophagocytic lymphohisiocytosis (HLH)

## Investigations

> Diagnosis of exclusion

- Elevated serum ferritin (> 3000 ng/ml)
- Low glycosylated ferritin (more specific)
- High ESR/CRP, leukocytosis (Neutrophilia)
- Autoantibody tests (ANA, RF): Usually negative
- Arthritis: Arthrocentesis for inflammatory profiles
- MAS: Bone marrow biopsies

## Management

> Depends on organ involvement and severity

### Mild-Moderate

1. First-ine: Corticosteroids
1. Adjuncts: NSAIDs
1. Steroid-sparing agents
   - Conventional DMARDs (methotrexate)
   - Biologics
     - IL-1 inhibitors (anakinra)
     - IL-6 inhibitors (tocilizumab)

### Severe

- Hospitalization, especially if MAS develops
