# Clinical Examination of the Respiratory System

import { Steps, Step } from "fumadocs-ui/components/steps";

<Steps>

<Step>

## General Observations

- Respiratory rate
- Fever, rash, cachexia
- Fetor
- Sputum
- Locale:
  - Oxygen delivery, Inhalers, Nebulizers

</Step>

<Step>

## Hands

- Finger clubbing
- Tar staining
- CO2 retention flap: Flapping tremor (Asterixis)
- Peripheral cyanosis
- Signs of occupation
- Radial pulse:
  - Rate: Increase in CO2 retention
  - Rhythm
- Blood pressure
  - Arterial paradox (Pulsus paradoxus)

</Step>

<Step>

## Head

- Jugular venous pulse:
  - Elevated
  - Pulsatile
- Pursed lips
- Central cyanosis
- Anaemia
- Horner syndrome

</Step>

<Step>

## Thorax

<Steps>

<Step>

### Inspection

- Deformity (e.g. pectus excavatum)
- Scars
- Intercostal indrawing
- Symmetry of expansion
- Hyperinflation
- Paradoxical rib movement (low flat diaphragm)

</Step>

<Step>

### Palpation

- From the front:
  - Trachea central
  - Cricosternal distance
  - Cardiac apex displaced
  - Expansion
- From behind:
  - Cervical lymphadenopathy
  - Expansion

</Step>

<Step>

### Percussion

- Resonant or dull
- 'Stony dull' (effusion)

</Step>

<Step>

### Auscultation

- Breath sounds:
  - normal, bronchial, louder or softer
- Added sounds:
  - wheezes, crackles, rubs
- Spoken voice (vocal resonance):
  - absent (effusion), increased (consolidation)
- Whispered voice:
  - whispering pectoriloquy

</Step>

</Steps>

</Step>

<Step>

## Leg Oedema

- Salt and water retention
- Cor pulmonale
- Venous thrombosis

</Step>

</Steps>
