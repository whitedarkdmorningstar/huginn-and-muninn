# Clinical Examination of Patients with Infectious Disease

import { Steps, Step } from "fumadocs-ui/components/steps";

<Steps>

<Step>

## General Observations

- Temperature
- Sweating
- Weight loss
- Respiratory distress
- Altered consciousness
- Pallor
- Jaundice

</Step>

<Step>

## Skin

- Generalized erythema
- Rash
- IV injection track marks
- Surgical scars
- Prosthetic devices e.g. central venous catheters
- Tattoos

</Step>

<Step>

## Hands and Nails

- Finger clubbing
- Splinter hemorrhages
- Janeway lesions
- Signs of chronic liver disease e.g. palmar erythema, leukonychia, koilonychia
- Vasculitic lesions e.g. purpura, petechiae, livedo reticularis

</Step>

<Step>

## Oropharynx

- Dental caries
- Tonsillar enlargement or exudate
- Candidiasis

</Step>

<Step>

## Head and Neck

- Lymphadenopathy
- Parotidomegaly
- Abnormal tympanic membrane

</Step>

<Step>

## Eyes

- Conjunctival petechiae
- Painful red eye in uveitis
- Loss or red reflex in endophthalmitis
- Roth spots in infective endocarditis
- Haemorrhages and exudates of cytomegalovirus retinitis
- Choroidal lesions in tuberculosis

</Step>

<Step>

## Neurological

- Neck stiffness
- Photophobia
- Delirium
- Focal neurological signs

</Step>

<Step>

## Heart and lungs

- Tachycardia, hypotension
- Murmurs or prosthetic heart sounds
- Pericardial rub
- Signs of consolidation
- Pleural or pericardial effusion

</Step>

<Step>

## Abdomen

- Hepatosplenomegaly
- Ascites
- Renal angle tenderness
- Localized tenderness or guarding with decreased bowel sounds e.g. in left iliac fossa in diverticulitis
- Mass lessions
- Surgical drains

</Step>

<Step>

## Musculoskeletal

- Joint swelling, erythema, tenderness
- Localized tender spine suggestive of epidual abscesses or discitis
- Draining sinus of chronic osteomyelitis

</Step>

<Step>

## Genitalia and rectum

- Ulceration or discharge
- Testicular swelling or nodules
- Inguinal lymphadenopathy
- Prostatic tenderness
- Rectal fluctuance

</Step>

</Steps>
