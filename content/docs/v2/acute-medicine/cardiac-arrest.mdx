# Cardiac Arrest

## Diagnosis

Confirm cadiac arrest if a patient has

1. No pulse
1. No respiration or abnormal breathing
1. No response (loss of conscious)

## Chain of Survival

1. Early recognition of cardiac arrest
1. Early CPR
1. Early defibrillation
1. Post arrest care

## BLS Healthcare Provider

1. Verify scene safety
1. Shut for nearby help; Activate emergency response system via mobile device; Get AED and emergency equipment
1. Check `breathing`, `pulse`
1. If `breathing` and `pulse` are **normal**:
   - Monitor until emergecy responders arrive
1. If `breathing` is ~~abnormal~~ but `pulse` is **normal**:
   - _Provide rescue breathing_:
     1. `1 breath every 5-6 seconds` or `about 10-12 breaths/min`
     1. Continue rescue breathing
     1. Check pulse about every 2 minutes
        - If ~~no pulse~~, begin _CPR_
   1. If possible opioid overdose, administer `naloxone` if available per protocol
1. If no `breathing` or only gasping, no `pulse`:
   1. _CPR_: `30 compressions and 2 breaths`
   1. If `AED` available, check **shockable rhythm**
      1. Give 1 shock if _shockable rhythm_
      1. Resume CPR immediately for about 2 minutes
      1. Continue until ALS providers take over or victim starts to move

## ALS

1. Start CPR
   - Give oxygen
   - Attach monitor/defibrillator
1. If _non-shockable rhythm_:
   - CPR 2 min (30:2)
   - IV/IO access
   - `Adrenaline IV 1 mg every 3-5 min`
   - Consider advanced airway, capnography
   - If _shockable rhythm_ exist, procceed as _shockable rhythm_
1. If _shockable rhythm_:
   - **First shock** (150-200 J biphasic **or** 360 J monophasic):
     - CPR 2 min
     - If still _shockable rhythm_:
       - **Second shock**:
         - CPR 2 min
         - `Adrenaline IV 1 mg every 3-5 min`
         - Consider advanced airway, capnography
         - If still _shockable rhythm_:
           - **Third shock**:
             - CPR 2 min
             - `Amiodarone 300 mg bolus fist dose; 150 mg further dose`
             - Treat eversible causes

### Reversible causes

| Medical condition             | Treatment                                    |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Hypovolemia                   | Fluid resuscitation                          |
| Hypoxia                       | Oxygen therapy                               |
| Hydrogen ion (acidosis)       |                                              |
| Hypo-/hyperkalaemia           | Treatment of hypo/hyperkalaemia and referral |
| Hpyothermia                   | Warming                                      |
| Tension pneumothorax          | Immediate decompression                      |
| Cardiac tamponade             | -                                            |
| Toxins                        | Treatment of intoxication                    |
| Pulmonary/coronary thrombosis | Rapid transfer to higher-level care          |
