# STEMI

## Management

import { Steps, Step } from "fumadocs-ui/components/steps";

<Steps>

<Step>

### Oxygen

- If SaO2 \<Step 90%

</Step>
<Step>

### ECG

- Record 12-lead ECG
- Attach ECG monitor

#### ECG criteria

1. ST elevation \> 1 mm in ≥ 2 adjacent limb leads \*_OR_ \> 2 mm in ≥ 2 adjacent chest leads
1. LBBB (unless prior LBBB), RBBB if ischaemic symptoms are present
1. Posterior changes: ST depression and tall R waves in leads V1 to V3

</Step>
<Step>

### Access

- FBC, U&E, troponin, glucose, lipids

</Step>
<Step>

### Assessment

- History: Risk factors for IHD
- Examination:
  - Pulse, BP (both arms), JVP,
  - Murmurs, signs of LVF
  - Scars from previous cardic surgery,
- CXR (if it does not delay treatment)
- Contraindications to PCI or fibrinolysis?

</Step>
<Step>

### Antiplatelets

- `PO Aspirin 300 mg`
- `PO Prasugrel 60 mg` (if not high bleeding risk) **OR** `PO Ticagrelor 180 mg` (if high bleeding risk) **OR**` PO Clopidogrel 300-600 mg` (if already taking an oral anticoagulant)

</Step>
<Step>

### Morphine

- `IV Morphine 2.5-5 mg` + anti-emetic (e.g. `IV metoclopramide 10 mg`) **OR**
- 2nd line `IV Cyclizine 50 mg`

</Step>
<Step>

### GTN

- `Sublingual GTN` (spray/tablet) if persistent chest discomfort/pain **OR**
- `IV GTN` if persistent symptoms + hyptertension **OR** LV dysfunction (Caution with hypotension or concurrent use of phosphodiesterase inhibitors e.g. `Sildenafil`)

</Step>
<Step>

### PCI or Fibrinolysis

- PCI available within 120 min?
- If _Yes_: `Primary PCI`
- If ~~No~~:
  - `Fibrinolysis`
  - Transfer to PCI centre for `rescue PCI` if fibinolysis unsuccessful **OR** `angiography`

#### Primary PCI

- Offer to all patients presenting with STEMI within **12 hours** of symptom onset
- If it is not possible, fibrinolysis and transfer to primar PCI centre for either rescue PCI (if residual ST elevation) or angiography

#### Fibrinolysis

- Target time is \< 10 min from diagnosis if PCI is not available
- Contraindications:
  - Previous ICH
  - Cerebral malignancy/AVM
  - Ischaemic stroke \< 6 months
  - Major trauma/surgery/head injury \< 3 weeks
  - GI bleed \< 2 months
  - Known bleeding disorde
  - Aortic dissection
  - Non-compressible puncture \< 24 hr (e.g. bipsy, LP)
- Relative contraindications:
  - TIA \< 6 months
  - Anticoagulant therapy
  - \< 1 week postpartum
  - Refractory BP \> 180/110 mmHg
  - Advanced liver disease
  - IE
  - Active PU
  - Prolonged/traumatic resuscitation
- Anticoagulation (e.g. `LMWH`) is recommended in patients treated with thrombolysis until revascularization or during hospital stay up to 8 days

</Step>
