## Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)

### Eye Opening (E) — max 4 points

- **4**: Spontaneous (opens eyes on own)
- **3**: To speech / sound (opens to verbal command)
- **2**: To pressure / pain (opens to noxious stimulus)
- **1**: None (no eye opening)

### Verbal Response (V) — max 5 points

- **5**: Oriented (converses, knows time, place, person)
- **4**: Confused (disoriented conversation, answers questions)
- **3**: Inappropriate words (exclamations, random words)
- **2**: Incomprehensible sounds (moans, groans)
- **1**: None (no verbal response)

### Motor Response (M) — max 6 points

- **6**: Obeys commands (follows multi-step/simple instructions)
- **5**: Localizes pain (purposeful movement toward pain)
- **4**: Withdraws from pain (pulls limb away from stimulus)
- **3**: Abnormal flexion / decorticate posture (rigid, arms flexed)
- **2**: Extension / decerebrate posture (rigid, arms and legs extended)
- **1**: None (no motor response)
