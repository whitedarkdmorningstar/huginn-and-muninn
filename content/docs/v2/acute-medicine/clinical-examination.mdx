# Clinical Examination in Critical Care

import { Steps, Step } from "fumadocs-ui/components/steps";

<Steps>
<Step>

## Airway

- Patent and Clear

</Step>

<Step>

## Breathing

- SpO2, Respiratory rate, Tidal volume
- Abnormal signs on chest examination
- Review arterial blood gas (ABG) and recent chest X-ray (CXR)

</Step>

<Step>

## Circulation

- Heart rate, blood pressure, peripheral temperature, [lactate](/docs/investigations/blood-tests/lactate), urine output
- Supports (inotropes, vasopressors)

</Step>

<Step>

## Disability

- Neurological assessment (GCS, pupillary response)
- Doses of sedative drugs

</Step>

<Step>

## Entral/exposure

- Input: feeding regimen
- Output: stool frequency
- Abdominal tenderness, bowel sounds

</Step>

<Step>

## Fluids, electrolytes and renal system

- Fluid balance
- Urine output, colour
- Any oedema
- Renal function and electrolytes

</Step>

<Step>

## Glucose

- Glucose levels
- Any insulin

</Step>

<Step>

## Haematology

- Haemoglobin, platelet count
- Any signs of bleeding

</Step>

<Step>

## Infection

- Temperature
- Recent infective markers (WCC, CRP, procalcitonin)
- Antibiotics: type, dose, duration

</Step>

</Steps>
